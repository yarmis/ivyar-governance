# Certification Risk

## Risk Factors

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Test program delays | 30% |
| Documentation gaps | 25% |
| Design changes | 25% |
| Regulatory issues | 20% |

## Scoring

| Score | Level |
|-------|-------|
| 0-30 | LOW |
| 31-60 | MEDIUM |
| 61-80 | HIGH |
| 81-100 | CRITICAL |

## Triggers

| Event | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Test finding | Retrofit |
| Doc incomplete | Delivery hold |
| Design change | Re-cert required |
