## Ministry Presentation

### Short Version (1 paragraph)
IVYAR is a national logistics intelligence platform designed to modernize military supply chain operations. It provides real-time visibility into parts availability, repair readiness, and operational risk — enabling faster decisions, better resource allocation, and full transparency for government oversight.

### Extended Version (3 paragraphs)
IVYAR addresses critical challenges in defense logistics: fragmented catalogs, manual risk tracking, and limited visibility into fleet readiness.

The platform integrates parts catalog management, automated risk scoring, GAP analysis, and predictive analytics into a single governance framework — aligned with NATO standards and international best practices.

IVYAR is designed for government deployment with full documentation, 5-level validation, audit readiness, and dedicated training programs. It supports ministry reporting, international donor transparency, and OEM partner integration.
