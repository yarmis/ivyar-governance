# IVYAR Go-To-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

Launch IVYAR as the standard military logistics platform for Ukraine and NATO partners.

## Target Segments

| Segment | Priority | Approach |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Ministry of Defence | P1 | Direct engagement |
| NATO Partners | P1 | Official channels |
| Donors | P2 | Presentations |
| OEM Partners | P2 | Business development |

## Phase 1: Foundation (Q1)

- Ministry adoption
- Core team onboarding
- Pilot deployment
- Success metrics

## Phase 2: Expansion (Q2)

- NATO integration
- Donor reporting
- OEM onboarding
- Feature expansion

## Phase 3: Scale (Q3-Q4)

- Regional deployment
- Full OEM network
- Analytics expansion
- International partners
