## 1. Overview

IVYAR operates as an institutional-grade platform providing governance, security, compliance, and operational capabilities to governments, defense organizations, and international partners.

### Revenue Model Summary

| Stream | Contribution | Description |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| SaaS Subscriptions | 55-60% | Annual platform licenses |
| Modular Add-ons | 20-25% | Security, Academy, Simulation |
| Transaction Fees | 10-15% | Procurement commissions |
| Professional Services | 5-10% | Deployment, training, support |

## 4. Pricing Model

### 4.1 Annual Subscriptions (SaaS)

Tiered pricing based on organization size and requirements.

| Tier | Annual Fee | Users | Includes |
|------|------------|-------|----------|
| **Starter** | $75,000 | Up to 50 | Core Platform (Governance + Catalog) |
| **Professional** | $150,000 | Up to 200 | + Procurement + Finance + Basic Security |
| **Enterprise** | $250,000 | Up to 500 | + Full Security Suite + Compliance |
| **National** | $500,000+ | Unlimited | Full Platform + Custom Modules |

### 4.2 Segment-Specific Pricing

Adjusted pricing for different customer segments.

| Segment | Typical Tier | Annual Range |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Small Government Agency | Starter | $75,000 - $100,000 |
| Ministry / Department | Professional - Enterprise | $150,000 - $350,000 |
| Defense Enterprise | Enterprise - National | $250,000 - $500,000 |
| International Partner | Enterprise - National | $250,000 - $500,000 |
| OEM Manufacturer | Professional - Enterprise | $150,000 - $300,000 |

### 4.3 Modular Add-ons

Optional modules purchased in addition to base subscription.

| Module | Annual Fee | Prerequisites |
|--------|------------|---------------|
| Security Suite (Full) | $50,000 - $100,000 | Professional+ tier |
| Compliance Engine | $40,000 | Starter+ tier |
| Simulation Engine | $50,000 | Enterprise+ tier |
| Academy Module | $20,000 | Any tier |
| Finance Engine (Advanced) | $30,000 | Professional+ tier |
| Multi-Agent AI | $75,000 | Enterprise+ tier |
| Ethical AI Governance | $40,000 | Enterprise+ tier |

### 4.4 Transaction Fees

Commission on procurement transactions processed through the platform.

| Transaction Volume | Commission Rate |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Up to $1M | 3.0% |
| $1M - $10M | 2.0% |
| $10M - $50M | 1.5% |
| $50M+ | 1.0% (negotiable) |

**Note:** Transaction fees are invoiced monthly or quarterly based on actual volume.

### 4.5 International Deployments

Custom deployments for new country or regional implementations.

| Component | Fee | Notes |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Initial Deployment | $500,000 | One-time setup and configuration |
| Annual Support | $250,000/year | Ongoing maintenance and updates |
| Training Package | $100,000 | Staff onboarding and certification |
| Localization | $50,000 - $150,000 | Language, regulatory adaptation |

## 8. Social Impact Commitment

### 8.1 Sails of Hope Partnership

IVYAR maintains a formal commitment to social responsibility through its partnership with Sails of Hope, a registered 501(c)(3) charitable organization.

### 8.2 Commitment Structure

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Allocation | 10% of Net Annual Revenue |
| Calculation | Net Revenue = Gross Revenue - Operating Expenses |
| Payer | IVYAR LLC (not clients directly) |
| Recipient | Sails of Hope 501(c)(3) |

### 8.3 Payment Schedule

| Option | Frequency | Process |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| Quarterly | Every 3 months | Based on quarterly net revenue |
| Annual | Once per year | Based on audited annual results |

### 8.4 Documentation & Transparency

| Document | Purpose | Frequency |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Financial Statement | Revenue calculation basis | Quarterly/Annual |
| Audit Log | Transaction verification | Per payment |
| Transfer Confirmation | Payment receipt | Per payment |
| Impact Report | Use of funds summary | Annual |

### 8.5 Legal Framework

**Contract Language:**

> "Sails of Hope (501(c)(3)) receives 10% of IVYAR's net annual revenue. Payments are made by IVYAR LLC, not by clients directly. This arrangement ensures legal compliance, avoids conflicts of interest with government clients, and maintains clear separation between commercial operations and charitable activities."

### 8.6 Governance

- Payments are approved by IVYAR Board
- Independent audit verification available upon request
- No client is required or requested to contribute directly to Sails of Hope
- Social impact commitment is disclosed in annual reports
