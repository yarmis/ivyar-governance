# Principles & Ethics

## Purpose
Defines the ethical foundations and accountability principles governing all IVYAR operations.

## Ethical Foundations
- **Human Dignity** — Respect inherent dignity of all persons
- **Do No Harm** — Minimize unintended harm
- **Proportionality** — Actions proportionate to needs
- **Transparency** — Stakeholders understand decisions
- **Accountability** — Clear chains of responsibility

## Gospel Golden Rules Ethical Engine
Core principles:
1. Treat others as you would be treated
2. Do unto others as you would have them do unto you
3. Love your neighbor as yourself
4. Bear one another's burdens
5. Let justice roll down like waters

## Institutional Commitments
- To Governments: Sovereignty and compliance
- To Partners: Fair dealings and protection
- To Beneficiaries: Humanitarian priority
- To Public: Transparency and responsibility
