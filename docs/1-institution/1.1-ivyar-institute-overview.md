# IVYAR Institute Overview

## Role of the Institute
Central governing body for the IVYAR Governance Platform:
- Strategic direction and objectives
- Standards development and maintenance
- Quality assurance and certification
- Knowledge leadership through Academy

## Institutional Mandate
Develop, maintain, and govern the IVYAR autonomous governance platform for ministries, defense organizations, humanitarian agencies, and critical infrastructure operators.

## Key Functions
1. **Platform Development** — Core architecture and modules
2. **Standards & Compliance** — Governance frameworks
3. **Training & Education** — Academy programs
4. **Partner Relations** — Ministry and OEM engagement
5. **Research & Innovation** — Future capabilities
