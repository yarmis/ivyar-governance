# Governance Layers (1–11)

## Layer 1: Foundation
Core platform infrastructure and runtime environment

## Layer 2: Identity
Authentication, authorization, and identity management

## Layer 3: Data
Data management, storage, and ontology

## Layer 4: Compliance
Regulatory compliance verification (LegalGuard 3.0)

## Layer 5: Ethics
Ethical evaluation using Gospel Golden Rules (GGR-EAE)

## Layer 6: Risk
Risk assessment, scoring, and mitigation (Risk Engine 3.0)

## Layer 7: Workflow
Business process orchestration and automation

## Layer 8: Integration
External system connectivity and interoperability

## Layer 9: Analytics
Reporting, dashboards, and business intelligence

## Layer 10: Audit
Audit trail, evidence chain, and compliance verification

## Layer 11: Governance
Meta-governance, policy management, and oversight
