# Risk & Audit Model

## Risk Categories
- Operational Risk
- Compliance Risk
- Ethical Risk
- Security Risk
- Strategic Risk

## Risk Score Formula
```
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact × (1 - Mitigation)
```

## Risk Thresholds
| Score | Level | Response |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 1-4 | Low | Monitor |
| 5-9 | Medium | Mitigate |
| 10-15 | High | Immediate action |
| 16-25 | Critical | Executive escalation |

## Audit Types
1. Compliance Audit
2. Operational Audit
3. Security Audit
4. Ethics Audit

## Traceability Requirements
- Decision traceability
- Data lineage
- Compliance mapping
- Remediation tracking
