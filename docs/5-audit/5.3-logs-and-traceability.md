# Logs & Traceability

## Log Types
| Type | Content | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Access Logs | Authentication events | 2 years |
| Transaction Logs | Business operations | 7 years |
| Audit Logs | System changes | 10 years |
| Security Logs | Security events | 5 years |

## Traceability Requirements
- End-to-end transaction tracking
- Decision reasoning capture
- Data lineage documentation
- Change history preservation

## Retention Rules
- Minimum retention by law
- Extended retention for audits
- Secure deletion procedures
- Archive management
