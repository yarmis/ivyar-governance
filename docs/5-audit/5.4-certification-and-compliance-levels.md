# Certification & Compliance Levels

## Certification Tiers
| Tier | Name | Requirements |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Basic | Core compliance |
| 2 | Standard | Enhanced controls |
| 3 | Advanced | Full governance |
| 4 | Premier | Excellence standard |

## Requirements per Tier
### Tier 1 (Basic)
- Legal compliance verified
- Basic security controls
- Audit trail enabled

### Tier 2 (Standard)
- Tier 1 + Ethics Engine
- Risk management
- Regular audits

### Tier 3 (Advanced)
- Tier 2 + Full governance layers
- Continuous monitoring
- Third-party audits

### Tier 4 (Premier)
- Tier 3 + Excellence metrics
- Industry leadership
- Innovation contribution

## Renewal & Revocation
- Annual renewal required
- Continuous compliance monitoring
- Revocation for violations
