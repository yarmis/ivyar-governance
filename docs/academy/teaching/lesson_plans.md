# IVYAR TEACHING MATERIALS

## For Instructors

### Lesson Plan: Ethical Decision-Making
Duration: 90 minutes
1. Lecture (20 min)
2. Case Study (15 min)
3. Simulation: Public Humiliation Scenario (20 min)
4. Group Reflection (20 min)
5. Assessment (15 min)

### Lesson Plan: Governance Under Crisis
Duration: 90 minutes
1. Theory: Resource Allocation (20 min)
2. Case: Fuel Shortage (15 min)
3. Simulation: Crisis Scenario (25 min)
4. Discussion: Ethical Tradeoffs (20 min)
5. Debrief (10 min)

### Assessment Criteria
- Understanding of concepts
- Ethical reasoning quality
- Decision justification
- Metrics interpretation
