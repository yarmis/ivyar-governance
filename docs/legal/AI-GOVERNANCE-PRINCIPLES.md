# IVYAR AI GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

## Overview
Principles for responsible AI governance within IVYAR Platform.

## 1. Transparency
All models and architectures are open and understandable.

## 2. Accountability
IVYAR does not perform real governance functions.

## 3. Safety by Design
All AI concepts include safety considerations and risk limitations.

## 4. Ethical Boundaries
AI has no political purpose and does not create power structures.

## 5. Legal Alignment
Materials comply with U.S. Constitution and legal norms.

## 6. Non-Interference
IVYAR does not interfere with political or governmental processes.

## 7. Research Orientation
Platform is intended for research, education, and system design.

## Conclusion
These principles ensure responsible and ethical AI use within IVYAR.
