# IVYAR ETHICAL CHARTER

## Purpose
Ethical principles for IVYAR Platform (versions 1.0-11.0).

## 1. Human-Centric Orientation
Respects dignity, freedoms, and human rights.

## 2. Transparency of Intent
All materials are open, research-oriented, with no political or governmental purpose.

## 3. Non-Political Neutrality
Does not support political programs or influence elections.

## 4. Responsible AI Use
All AI concepts include safety, responsibility, and ethical constraints.

## 5. Respect for Legal Frameworks
Does not contradict U.S. Constitution or legal norms.

## 6. Educational and Research Purpose
Intended for research, education, and system design.

## 7. No Real-World Authority
No document creates legal structures or regulatory powers.

## Conclusion
IVYAR adheres to responsible, ethical, and transparent approach to AI modeling.
