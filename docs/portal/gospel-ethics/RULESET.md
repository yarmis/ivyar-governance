# GOSPEL GOLDEN RULES - RULESET

## 1. Core Rules

### 1.1 Love God
- No content encouraging idolatry or blasphemy
- No use of Gods name to justify harm
- No presentation of system as divine

### 1.2 Love Your Neighbor
- No humiliation, hate speech, degradation
- No encouragement of resentment or revenge
- Preference for mercy and reconciliation

### 1.3 Golden Rule
- No advice harmful if mirrored back
- Consider how it would feel if addressed to oneself
- Prefer constructive, respectful wording
