# HOW TO BUY IN IVYAR

## Procurement Workflow | RFQ | Quotes | PO | Tracking

**Version:** 1.0  
**For:** Ministries | Government Agencies | International Partners

---

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the complete procurement cycle for parts, assemblies, and materials in the IVYAR system.

## 2. Procurement Workflow

1. Search for part
2. Review technical information
3. Add to RFQ Cart
4. Send RFQ to suppliers
5. Receive and compare Quotes
6. Create Purchase Order
7. Track delivery
8. Close order

## 3. Searching for Parts

Supported search types:
- NSN / NIIN
- OEM Part Number
- Description search
- Cross-reference search

## 4. RFQ (Request for Quote)

RFQ Cart allows:
- Add items
- Modify quantities
- Add comments
- Send requests to suppliers

## 5. Quotes

Suppliers respond with:
- Price
- Lead time
- Terms
- Certificates
- Country of origin
- Risk indicators

## 6. Purchase Order

PO includes:
- Part details
- Quantity
- Price
- Terms
- Signatory
- Status

## 7. Order Tracking

Statuses:
- Pending
- Confirmed
- Shipped
- Delivered

## 8. Audit and Compliance

All actions:
- Logged with timestamps
- Assigned unique IDs
- Accessible to auditors
- Compliant with governance requirements
