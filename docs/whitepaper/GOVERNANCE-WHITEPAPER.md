## 1. Introduction

IVYAR is an institutional platform for managing logistics readiness, risk, procurement, and international coordination. This document describes the governance model that ensures transparency, accountability, and compliance with international standards.

## 2. Governance Principles

- Accountability
- Transparency
- Integrity
- Compliance
- Security
- Auditability

## 3. Governance Architecture

IVYAR uses a multi-layer model:
- Policy Layer
- Approval Layer
- Audit Layer
- Compliance Layer
- Operational Layer

## 4. Policy Framework

- Versioned policies
- Controlled updates
- Ministerial oversight
- Immutable documentation

## 5. Approval Workflows

- Multi-level approvals
- Role-based routing
- Compliance gates
- Full traceability

## 6. Audit and Evidence

- Immutable logs
- Evidence export
- Decision path reconstruction
- Auditor dashboards

## 7. Compliance Alignment

- Export control
- Sanctions screening
- NATO STANAG compatibility
- Donor transparency

## 8. Risk Governance

- Partner risk
- Shipment risk
- Export control risk
- Operational risk

## 9. Readiness Governance

- Readiness index
- Stock visibility
- Deployment readiness

## 10. Conclusion

IVYAR establishes a new standard for institutional transparency, accountability, and international compatibility.
