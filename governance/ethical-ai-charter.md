# IVYAR Ethical AI & Humanitarian Principles Charter

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-01  
**Classification:** Official

---

## 1. Core Ethical Principles

### 1.1. Human-Centric AI

- ML models serve humans
- Explainability by default
- Human control over decisions

### 1.2. Do-No-Harm

- No use for human rights violations
- No use for repression
- No use for illegal operations

### 1.3. Neutrality & Impartiality

- Political neutrality
- Equal access
- Non-discrimination

### 1.4. Transparency

- Audit logs
- Explainability
- Oversight board

### 1.5. Accountability

- ML responsibility
- Data responsibility
- Decision responsibility
