## 1. Mission

IVYAR is built for transparent, controlled, and auditable defence and humanitarian procurement.

Our platform enables governments, international organizations, and authorized partners to conduct procurement with full visibility, accountability, and compliance.

---

## 2. Transparency Principles

### ✅ Full Auditability

Every action on the platform is logged with:
- User identification
- Timestamp
- Action details
- Affected resources

### ✅ Immutable Logs

Audit logs cannot be modified or deleted:
- Cryptographic signatures
- Append-only storage
- Third-party verification

### ✅ Oversight Access

Independent oversight boards have access to:
- All audit trails
- Compliance reports
- Risk assessments
- ML model outputs

### ✅ Version Control

Every document maintains:
- Complete revision history
- Change attribution
- Rollback capability

### ✅ Open Architecture

Transparent systems for:
- Catalog management
- ML classification
- Risk scoring
- Compliance checks
