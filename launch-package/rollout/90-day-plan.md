# 90-Day Rollout Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

- Week 1: System live
- Week 2: 100 users onboarded
- Week 3: Bugs fixed
- Week 4: Month 1 report

## Phase 2: Expansion (Days 31-60)

- Week 5: 200 users
- Week 6: Features updated
- Week 7: Training complete
- Week 8: Month 2 report

## Phase 3: Optimization (Days 61-90)

- Week 9: 500 users
- Week 10: OEM connected
- Week 11: Dashboards live
- Week 12: Quarter report

## Milestones

- Day 1: Go-live
- Day 30: Stabilized
- Day 60: Expanded
- Day 90: Optimized

## Success Criteria

- 500 active users
- 99.5% uptime
- Ministry approval
