## 1. Overview

IVYAR Inc. ("Company") is committed to full compliance with all applicable export control laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

- U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
- International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR)
- Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Sanctions
- EU Export Control Regulations

## 2. Classification

The IVYAR Platform is classified as:

| Classification | Value |
|----------------|-------|
| ECCN | EAR99 |
| ITAR Controlled | No |
| Encryption | Yes (AES-256) |

## 3. Prohibited Destinations

The IVYAR Platform may not be exported, re-exported, or transferred to:

- Countries subject to U.S. comprehensive sanctions
- Denied parties on U.S. government restricted party lists
- End-users engaged in prohibited activities

## 4. Screening Procedures

Company maintains the following screening procedures:

1. **Pre-contract screening** — All customers screened against restricted party lists
2. **Ongoing monitoring** — Continuous screening of existing customers
3. **Red flag review** — Investigation of suspicious activities

## 5. End-Use Restrictions

The IVYAR Platform may not be used for:

- Development of weapons of mass destruction
- Military applications in prohibited countries
- Human rights violations

## 6. Compliance Program

Company maintains a comprehensive export compliance program including:

- Designated Export Compliance Officer
- Regular training for employees
- Internal audits and assessments
- Record-keeping for required periods

Any suspected violations must be reported immediately to:
