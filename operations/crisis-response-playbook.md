---

## 3. Crisis Procurement Workflow

### Step 1: Rapid Needs Assessment

- Field data collection
- Logistics constraints evaluation
- Risk assessment

### Step 2: Emergency RFQ

- Pre-approved templates
- Automated compliance checks
- Automated risk scoring
- Expedited timelines

### Step 3: Supplier Activation

- Pre-qualified humanitarian suppliers
- OEM rapid response
- Certified logistics operators

### Step 4: Compliance & Licensing

- Expedited sanctions screening
- Dual-use classification
- Emergency export exemptions

### Step 5: Delivery & Verification

- Real-time tracking
- Priority customs clearance
- Field inspection
- Rapid acceptance

### Step 6: Audit & Reporting

- Immutable logging
- Oversight review
- Donor reporting
