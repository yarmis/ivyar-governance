# International Humanitarian Procurement Protocol (IHPP)

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-01  
**Classification:** Official

---

## 1. Mission

Enable transparent, rapid, and controlled humanitarian procurement during crisis, war, or emergency situations.

---

## 2. Humanitarian Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Speed | Minimize delays |
| Transparency | Complete audit trail |
| Neutrality | Political impartiality |
| Accountability | Oversight board |
| Compliance | Sanctions, export control |
| Protection | Personnel and cargo safety |

---

## 3. IHPP Procurement Workflow

### Step 1: Needs Assessment

- Field data collection
- Logistics constraints
- Risk evaluation

### Step 2: Rapid RFQ

- Pre-approved templates
- Automated compliance
- Automated risk scoring

### Step 3: Supplier Matching

- Humanitarian suppliers
- OEM partners
- Certified logistics operators

### Step 4: Compliance & Licensing

- Sanctions screening
- Dual-use classification
- Export control
- Emergency exemptions

### Step 5: Delivery & Verification

- Real-time tracking
- Customs clearance
- Inspection
- Acceptance

### Step 6: Audit & Reporting

- Immutable logs
- Oversight review
- Donor reporting

---

## 4. Emergency Procedures

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Active Conflict | Expedited processing |
| Natural Disaster | Simplified RFQ |
| Health Emergency | Priority procurement |
