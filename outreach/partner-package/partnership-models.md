# Partnership Models

## Model Types

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Catalog Partner | Data integration |
| Technology Partner | Platform development |
| Funding Partner | Financial support |
| Strategic Partner | Long-term alliance |

## Catalog Partner

- Provide parts data
- Receive market access
- Co-branding allowed
- Annual renewal

## Technology Partner

- Build integrations
- Joint development
- Shared roadmap
- Revenue share

## Funding Partner

- Financial support
- Impact reporting
- Visibility rights
- Advisory role

## Strategic Partner

- Full collaboration
- Joint planning
- Shared resources
- Long-term commitment

## Terms

- 12-month minimum
- Quarterly reviews
- Clear deliverables
- Exit clause included
