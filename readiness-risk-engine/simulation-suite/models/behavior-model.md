# Behavior Model

## Risk Escalation

| Days Without Parts | Risk Increase |
|--------------------|---------------|
| 0-7 | +5 points |
| 8-14 | +10 points |
| 15-21 | +15 points |
| 22+ | +20 points |

## Readiness Decay

| Condition | Daily Decay |
|-----------|-------------|
| Normal ops | -0.5% |
| Parts shortage | -2% |
| Critical shortage | -5% |

## Recovery Rate

| Action | Recovery |
|--------|----------|
| Parts delivered | +3% daily |
| OEM contract | +5% weekly |
| Emergency stock | +10% immediate |
