# IVYAR Unified Dual-Domain Strategy

## Executive Summary

IVYAR operates across two distinct domains that share architectural principles but never overlap in data, operations, or interests.

## Domain Overview

| Domain | Focus | Status |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Military | Defense logistics | PRIMARY |
| Aerospace | Aviation operations | SECONDARY |

## Core Principle

Mutual reinforcement without intersection.

## Shared Elements

- Modular architecture
- Risk engine framework
- Simulation methodology
- Dashboard patterns
- Governance models

## Separated Elements

- Data sources
- Stakeholders
- Operations
- Compliance
- Funding

## Strategic Value

- Military strengthened by aerospace discipline
- Aerospace informed by operational flexibility
- Both domains independent and protected
